# Changelog

What changed in each version, written for the people who use it. Newest version at the top.

## Not released yet
### Added
- Ten more profile questions: height, body type, skin colour, eye colour, hair colour, ethnic background, tattoos, highest education, who you live with and relationship status. The profile page now shows every answer.
- Search profiles: describe the people you are looking for, as many searches as you like (up to 20), and edit or delete them.
- The matches page ranks members by how well they fit the chosen search, from the highest % down, 20 a page.
- When someone new matches one of your searches at 80% or more, a notification appears on the bell in the header, live, and arrives by email too.
### Changed
- Emails go through SolarFramework's mailer, like MauCareers.
### Fixed
- Interests and languages can be picked, unpicked and cleared again, including with the keyboard.
- Saving your profile without a new photo no longer removes the current one.
- A profile form can only ever change your own profile.
- Uploading a file that isn't a picture is refused with a message.
- The matches page showed a fixed 10% and broken photos.
- "What studies did you do?" is back on the profile form.
- An unknown profile address shows "not found" instead of an error.
- Saving a profile, saving or deleting a search, and every chat action no longer fail with an error page.
- Pages show what was just saved, instead of the old values for up to an hour, and the chat list and reaction counts update at once.
- Profile photos show on match cards, the profile page and the header, and a new photo appears straight away.
- Emptying "How many previous partners" no longer loses the whole form.
